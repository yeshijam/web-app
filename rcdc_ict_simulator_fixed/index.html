Institutional Mandate & Quality Management

Organizational Background and Overview of the HP-ICT Test Kit Production Unit

Serbithang, Thimphu • First Floor, RCDC

The Royal Centre for Disease Control (RCDC), formerly known as the Public Health Laboratory (PHL), was established in 1989 under the Ministry of Health, Royal Government of Bhutan, and relocated to its current premises in Serbithang, Thimphu, in 2015. RCDC serves as the national centre of excellence for disease control, public health laboratory services, research, disease surveillance, and the development of diagnostic technologies. Its mandate includes providing specialized laboratory services, conducting research, strengthening disease surveillance, and supporting national disease prevention and control programmes.

In line with its mandate, RCDC established the Helicobacter pylori Immunochromatographic Test Kit Production Unit (HP-ICT Test Kit Production Unit) under the Project for Institutional Capacity Building for Eliminating H. pylori-Related Deaths in Bhutan. The Unit is located on the first floor of the RCDC building and is responsible for the production, inspection, packaging, labelling, storage, and distribution of HP-ICT Test Kits. It operates under the overall management of the Specialist Head of RCDC and the supervision of the designated Quality Manager and Head of the Production Unit. The legal identity of the Unit is provided in Annexure 1.

The Production Unit is equipped with the necessary infrastructure, equipment, and qualified personnel to ensure that its activities are carried out under controlled conditions. Personnel competence is maintained through appropriate training, competency assessments, and professional development. Through the implementation and continual improvement of its Quality Management System (QMS), the Unit is committed to ensuring that its products consistently meet specified quality, safety, performance, and applicable statutory and regulatory requirements.
